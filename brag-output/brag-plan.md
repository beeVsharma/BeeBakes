# Brag Plan: Bee Bakes by Vartika

## What is this app?

Bee Bakes is a Bikaner bakery landing page for custom eggless cakes, artisan cookies, and dessert boxes — ordered over WhatsApp with a calm, clean finish.

## The angle

A quiet premium bakery reveal. No hype, no fake startup launch — the page's own voice is "clean, elegant, eggless." The video shows real cakes (London Viral caramel, Tiramisu, Biscoff cheesecake), real prices in rupees, real copy ("baked fresh in Bikaner"), and the three-step WhatsApp ordering flow. The joke is restrained: this is a bakery that takes itself seriously, and that IS the charm.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

A warm cream-on-dark title card: giant serif "Bee Bakes" + tiny italic gold "by Vartika". A single line underneath: "Custom cakes with a calmer, cleaner finish." A soft gold halo breathes behind the wordmark. The hook is the brand promise — calm before the cake.

## Key moments (the middle)

- **The cakes.** Three real product images enter one by one (London Viral Whipped Caramel → Tiramisu → Biscoff Cheesecake), each with its actual name + price (Rs 700 / Rs 600 / Rs 800). Beat-locked card reveals with a soft "place" sound each.
- **Eggless, fresh, Bikaner.** A stat strip: "100% Eggless builds" / "Baked close to delivery" / "Bikaner, Rajasthan". Counted-in, honest, no decoration overload.
- **Three-step ordering.** The working flow as a phone-card mock: 1 Choose your cake → 2 Share the details → 3 WhatsApp confirmation. Simulated interaction: a WhatsApp confirmation bubble pops in at the end. This is the product in use — ordering, not just the landing page.

## Outro / punchline

One held line over the dark board: "Order on WhatsApp." Then the logo locks: "Bee Bakes — by Vartika" with a dry single logo-hit SFX and the music fading under it.

## User flow worth showing

Entry → key action → result:

- Browse the range (cake cards enter)
- Choose / custom order (three-step card, step highlights advance)
- WhatsApp confirmation bubble arrives → done

## Tone

- Preset: **polished**
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film for a small-city bakery
- Interpretation: few scenes, longer holds, confidence through restraint. Cream/gold/cocoa palette straight from the site CSS variables. Serif display for warmth, sans body for clarity. Soft crossfades and gentle wipes. Reads as "we spent care, not money."

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080

## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)

- Background: `#fbf7f1` (light cream) for most scenes; `#2f221b` (deep cocoa) for hook/outro frames
- Accent: `#9f6636` (gold)
- Text: `#2f221b` (dark cocoa) on light; `#fbf7f1` on dark
- Muted text: `#6f5846`
- Display font: a warm serif (Georgia/"Playfair Display"-class) — matches the site's italic gold headings
- Body font: system sans (site uses default sans stack)
- Strongest visual element: the cake cards with the gold italic section labels ("Featured Cakes", "Simple ordering")

## Share copy (draft)

Bee Bakes by Vartika — custom eggless cakes, ordered on WhatsApp, baked fresh in Bikaner. London Viral caramel to Biscoff cheesecake, Rs 220–800. Cake first. Copy second.

## Audio direction

- Role: warm premium bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (114.84 BPM)
- Music treatment: start immediately, warm full-bed posture, fade out under the final logo hit
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset read — strong cues at 3.70s, 6.34s, 10.54s, 12.65s. Hook locks at ~3.70s. Cake card reveals lock to 6.34s, 8.44s, 10.54s. Ordering flow snap to beats from ~13.18s. Final logo locks to ~23.17s.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; use music RMS/bass to make the hero halo and cake-card presence breathe. No waveform/equalizer visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched; a soft card "place" per cake reveal, a subtle click for the WhatsApp confirmation bubble, one dry logo hit at the end
- Audio-coupled moments: cake cards landing (beat + card sound), WhatsApp bubble arriving (soft click), final logo (single hit, music ducks under)
- Restraint rule: never louder than the brand — no whooshes, no glitchy bursts, no chatty stingers

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Brand lockup — 3.5s (0.0–3.5)

Dark cocoa board. Gold halo breathes in behind. Serif "Bee Bakes" rises in, italic gold "by Vartika" beneath, then one line: "Custom cakes with a calmer, cleaner finish."
Sequential/interaction: yes — halo, then wordmark, then subline, each beat-aligned
Audio intent: warm open, soft swell
Audio-coupled idea: halo + wordmark land on the strong cue at ~3.70s
Music: warm bed intro
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The cakes — 6.5s (3.5–10.0)

Light cream board, gold italic label "Featured Cakes". Three real product cards enter one by one, each with name + rupee price, landing on beats.
Sequential/interaction: yes — card 1 (London Viral Whipped Caramel Cream Cake, Rs 700), card 2 (Tiramisu, Rs 600), card 3 (Biscoff Cheesecake, Rs 800), ~0.6s apart
Audio intent: rhythmic, satisfying placements
Audio-coupled idea: each card lands on a beat with a soft card sound
Music: bed picks up
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The promise — 4.0s (10.0–14.0)

Same cream board. Three honest stats count in one by one: "100% Eggless builds" / "Baked close to delivery" / "Bikaner, Rajasthan". Tabular numbers.
Sequential/interaction: yes — stat rows slide in sequentially
Audio intent: calm credibility
Audio-coupled idea: rows on beats, subtle ticks
Music: steady bed
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The flow — 4.0s (14.0–18.0)

Light board, gold label "Simple ordering". Three numbered steps stacked as a phone-card: "1 Choose your cake" / "2 Share the details" / "3 WhatsApp confirmation." The word "WhatsApp" is emphasized. A WhatsApp confirmation bubble slides in at the end.
Sequential/interaction: yes — steps advance one by one; a simulated confirmation bubble arrives
Audio intent: light, easy, resolved
Audio-coupled idea: steps snap to beats; bubble arrives with a soft click
Music: bed with a subtle lift into the outro
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Logo out — 2.0s (18.0–20.0)

Dark cocoa board returns. "Order on WhatsApp." Then logo locks: "Bee Bakes — by Vartika" with gold accent. Music ducks, one dry logo hit.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: close, confident, quiet
Audio-coupled idea: logo locks on the strong cue at ~19.5–20s; single logo hit, music fades under
Transition mood: end
